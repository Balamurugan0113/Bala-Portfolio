# INTELLIQ — Association of AI & Data Science
Official web platform for the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at INFO Institute of Engineering, Coimbatore.

## Features
- **Department Showcases**: Public portfolio of student technical accomplishments, faculty publications, and guest speaker archives.
- **Event Management**: Live symposium schedules, workshop agendas, and quota-controlled attendee registrations.
- **Transactional Safety**: PostgreSQL relational integrity with ACID compliance and Pydantic validation.
